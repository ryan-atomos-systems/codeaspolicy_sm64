[Nerfies website](https://nerfies.github.io/) by the Nerfies authors.
Template source: <https://github.com/nerfies/nerfies.github.io>, commit
`657409a62d59a93163872c0e4921cf651b987810` (retrieved September 28, 2026).

are not included. The footer preserves the template attribution.
